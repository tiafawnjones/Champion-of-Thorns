About the book

A new world. Deadly Trials. A choice that could change everything.

Running for her life, Tressa Sterne crosses into Averen, a world of rival Fae Courts and deadly Trials. As she fights to survive a realm she never knew existed, she encounters Cairn, a man whose secrets may be as dangerous as the forces hunting her.
